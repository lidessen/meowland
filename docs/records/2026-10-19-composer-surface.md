## Shared structural correction

`ChatComposer` keeps one native `form` at `chat-composer/root`, without surface chrome. A new `chat-composer/surface` div contains attachments, field/input and actions. The hint remains inside the same form, after and outside that surface. `hintId` continues to associate the instructions with the textarea through `aria-describedby`; the host still owns draft state, actual submission, model facts and any keyboard shortcut implementation.

The standard stacked layout keeps draft above the bottom action row on desktop. Its existing narrow layout keeps attach, input and send on the main writing row, with model selection below. The supporting hint follows outside the rounded edge, in normal flow. Long text must wrap without forcing controls outside their allocated surface. Focus chrome belongs to the real writing surface, not to the outer form containing instructions.

`ComposerPanel` retains its existing `composer-panel/surface` wrapper for composition compatibility, but that wrapper does not paint another surface. Its inner `ChatComposer` uses the default, unique writing surface; the external hint follows that surface, and the optional page-level meta slot follows the Composer. This avoids two layers of chrome or a separate consumer implementation.

Inline layout retains the one-line input/action alignment and exposes supporting instructions outside its surface while focused. Hero's existing visually hidden hint behavior remains unchanged. Explicit `surface="none"` removes the inner surface chrome. The correction does not add shortcut execution, hide important shared hints, change package names, introduce app-specific absolute positioning or alter the overall palette.

The footer keyboard regression follows the new DOM order: input, attachment action, send, then the external help link. Native textarea Enter still inserts a newline unless the host implements different behavior; fixture instructions describe that behavior honestly.
