# Image and recording sources

All assets in this work are original Meowland work under the repository's MIT license; there are no stock libraries, external generation services, third-party fonts or music.

- `coast-original.png`: the deterministic coast illustration generated by `../scripts/generate-images.ts`, 2200×1400, 3,634,345 bytes. Not photography or network-fact evidence.
- `coast-delivery.jpg`: FFmpeg `-q:v 14` JPEG encoding of that PNG, same dimensions, 68,426 bytes; fine lines and edges lose some detail, and no claim is made that quality is identical or that the ratio is universal.
- `local-transfer.webm`: records a real local HTTP request and decode via `../scripts/record-transfer.ts`, silent VP8, about 12 seconds; controlled chunked sending is illustrative only and is not a public-network baseline. See [recording.json](recording.json) for actual conditions and results.
- `local-transfer.vtt`: original English captions; the evidence page also has an always-visible text alternative.

Estimates and display use decimal bytes/kB/MB/Mbit/s and ignore latency, server, decoding, caching and variation; a warm cache may avoid the transfer. The local system sans-serif font is not distributed with the artifact.

The original standalone test tone has moved to Meowslides' PresentationAudio story and is no longer an asset of this work; its synthesis method and license are retained in the component story's assets/NOTICES.md.
