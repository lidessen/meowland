# Sources and artwork

`community.svg`, `sight.svg`, `hearing.svg`, `access.svg`, `older.svg`, `home.svg` and `needs.svg` are original Meowland vector artwork created for this lesson, under the repository MIT license. They are illustrations and learning symbols, not textbook images or documentary photographs. No external images, audio, fonts or music are used.

The lesson is a teacher-created adaptation for English 8B Unit 6, Welcome to the unit. The Oxford/Yilin 2011-system textbook vocabulary and source scope were checked by the task research and independent content reviewers; the exact access boundary is recorded in SOURCES.md at the editable lesson root and browser delivery root. No textbook page screenshot, full dialogue or answer key is copied into the work.
